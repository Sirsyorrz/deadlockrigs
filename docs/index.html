Deadlock Rigs

A Blender add-on that builds animation rigs of Deadlock heroes, NPCs and maps from your own game install.

Needs Blender 5.1 or newer and Deadlock installed through Steam.

  1. Drag the button for your platform into an open Blender window.
  2. The first time, Blender asks to add the Deadlock Rigs repository: press Add Repository and tick Check for Updates on Startup, then drag the button in again and press Install. From then on Blender offers updates in Edit > Preferences > Get Extensions.
  3. Already installed from a zip? Uninstall it in Get Extensions first. Zips installed from disk don't get updates.
  4. Open the sidebar in the 3D View (N) > Deadlock Rigs, pick a hero and press Build Rig.
